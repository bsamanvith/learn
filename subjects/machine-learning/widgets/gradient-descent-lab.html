Walking downhill on a loss surface

Fitting a line ŷ = w·x + b to the points on the left is the same as finding the lowest spot on the map on the right. Every spot on the map is one line. Click the map to pick a starting line, then press Run and watch gradient descent walk down to the best fit.

The orange trail is every (w, b) the walk visited. The arrow is the next step, −η·∇L. It always points downhill.

Try this: press Too big and watch the walk jump across the valley further each time until it diverges. Then switch the data to Raw x: the bowl becomes a long narrow valley, the safe learning rate shrinks, and the walk zig-zags and crawls. That is why we centre and scale inputs before training.
