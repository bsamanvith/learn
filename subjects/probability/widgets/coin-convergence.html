Does the fraction of heads settle down?

Each line is one person flipping a coin over and over, tracking the running fraction of heads. The shaded funnel is where we expect almost all lines to be (p ± 2·√(p(1−p)/n)).

Notice: the lines never stop wiggling, but the wiggles shrink like 1/√n. To halve the typical error you need four times as many flips. That's the law of large numbers (Part 6) and its rate, the central limit theorem, in one picture.
