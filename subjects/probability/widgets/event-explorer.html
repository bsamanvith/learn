Events are just sets of outcomes

Roll two dice. The sample space Ω is all 36 (first, second) pairs — each square below. An event is any set of squares. Build events with "or", "and", "not", and compare two expressions side by side.

Faint colours show where A, B and both live; bright squares are the selected event. Try: is (A ∪ B)ᶜ the same as Aᶜ ∩ Bᶜ? What about (A ∩ B)ᶜ and Aᶜ ∪ Bᶜ? (De Morgan's laws.) Find A and B that are disjoint.
