The hat-check problem

n people check their hats. The attendant hands them back in a uniformly random order. What's the chance that nobody gets their own hat (a derangement)?

P(nobody gets their own hat) vs n

How many people get their own hat? (simulated vs Poisson(1))

Inclusion–exclusion gives P(no match) = 1 − 1/1! + 1/2! − 1/3! + ··· ± 1/n!, the start of the series for e−1 ≈ 0.3679. The terms shrink factorially fast, so by n = 6 the answer is already 1/e to three decimals. The number of matches is close to Poisson with mean 1 (a preview of Part 3) — and its expectation is exactly 1 for every n.
