Counting paths two ways

Walk from the bottom-left corner to the top-right, one step right or up at a time. Each dot shows how many routes reach it. Every route crosses the purple diagonal at exactly one dot — so splitting by crossing point counts the same routes a second way.

Each number is the sum of the one to its left and the one below (you arrive from one or the other) — that's Pascal's rule, and tilting the grid 45° gives Pascal's triangle. The diagonal split is Vandermonde's identity.
