The ménage problem

n couples sit round a table, alternating women and men. The women are seated; the men then sit at random in the gaps. How likely is it that nobody sits next to their partner?

P(nobody next to their partner) against n

Distance from the limit (log scale): ménage vs hat-check

Circles are women, squares are men, and the number is the couple. A red link marks a couple sitting together. Each man has two forbidden seats, so the limit is e−2 ≈ 0.1353, against e−1 for the hat-check problem, where each person has one forbidden hat. But the hat-check answer closes in on its limit like 1/n!, and the ménage answer only like 1/n: P ≈ e−2(1 − 1/n). The forbidden seats of neighbouring men overlap, and that overlap fades slowly.
