Area is probability

The square is the sample space Ω with total area 1. Throw a dart uniformly at random: the chance it lands in an event is that event's area. Drag the circles; resize them with the sliders.

Adding P(A) and P(B) counts the purple overlap twice, so subtract it once. With three events, pairwise overlaps are subtracted — but then the centre (A∩B∩C) has been added 3 times and removed 3 times, so it's added back. That alternating pattern is inclusion–exclusion.
